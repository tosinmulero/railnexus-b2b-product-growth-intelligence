# RailNexus Project Brief

## Target role alignment

RailNexus is designed around a Senior Product Data Scientist role embedded in a B2B rail product team. It prioritises product influence and business impact rather than model complexity for its own sake.

The project deliberately demonstrates:

- product engagement evaluation;
- measurable success criteria and metric trees;
- A/B experimentation;
- ambiguous multi-source analysis;
- B2B partner activation and growth;
- cross-functional decision support;
- geospatial and graph opportunity discovery;
- parametric and non-parametric predictive modelling;
- AI-assisted analytical automation;
- clear visual communication.

## Fictional business context

RailNexus is a fictional B2B rail technology platform. Its partners include:

- corporate travel platforms;
- online travel retailers;
- rail carriers.

Partners can integrate through API, embedded widget or white-label products.

The primary growth problem is to increase successful rail bookings and repeat partner usage without degrading reliability, latency or downstream refund/cancellation outcomes.

## North-star metric

**Successful booking value per active partner**

This balances commercial value with actual partner adoption.

## Supporting metrics

Activation:
- integration completion rate;
- first-search rate;
- first-booking rate;
- 30-day activation rate.

Engagement:
- weekly active partners;
- searches per active partner;
- repeat-booking rate;
- feature adoption.

Conversion:
- search-to-result;
- result-to-selection;
- search-to-book;
- no-result rate.

Experiment guardrails:
- search latency;
- checkout/API error rate;
- cancellation rate;
- refund rate.

Growth:
- partner retention;
- partner expansion;
- route coverage;
- underserved demand opportunity score.
